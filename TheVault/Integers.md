> no concept of unsigned int

#### `SMALLINT`: 
- Range :  [ -32768  32767]
- `int2` → alias of `smallint`, more informative as it tells that it consists of 2 bytes

#### `INTEGER`
- `int4` → is also called `INTEGER`
- It's also the default integer data-type
- Range: [ -2,147,483,648  2,147,483,647 ]


#### `BIGINT`
- `int8` →  alias of `BIGINT`
- Range: [ -9,223,372,036,854,775,808  9,223,372,036,854,775,807 ]

> `int2`, `int4` and `int8` are integers  → Fast and they only support whole numbers (without any decimal places)
